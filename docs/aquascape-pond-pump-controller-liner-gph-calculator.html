How do I calculate the exact pond liner size for Aquascape Pond Pump Controller?

Use the standard excavation formula: Liner Length = Pond Length (14.0') + (2 × Max Depth [3.0']) + 2 ft overlap = 22 feet. Liner Width = Pond Width (10.0') + (2 × Max Depth [3.0']) + 2 ft overlap = 18 feet. Order a 18' × 22' 45-mil EPDM sheet.

How many GPH pump do I need for a 2,510-gallon pond with an 18-inch waterfall?

A healthy Koi pond requires turning over the entire 2,510-gallon volume at least once per hour, while an 18-inch waterfall weir requires 2,250 GPH (125 GPH per inch of width) for a full cascading sheet. Accounting for 25% vertical head and pipe friction loss, choose a pump rated around 3,100 GPH at 0-ft head.

Should I use 45-mil EPDM or 20-mil PVC/HDPE liner for Aquascape Pond Pump Controller?

Professional pond builders exclusively recommend 45-mil Fish-Safe EPDM synthetic rubber. Unlike stiff 20-mil PVC (which plasticizer-hardens and cracks under UV sunlight in 5–7 years), 45-mil EPDM stretches 300% around shelf contours and lasts 30 to 40+ years.
